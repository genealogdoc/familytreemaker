How to Set Up Your Family Tree Maker Login Account
====================================================

Starting your family history journey with Family Tree Maker is an exciting step, and understanding the different ways to access your Family Tree Maker login account is essential for a smooth experience. Unlike a single website login, Family Tree Maker uses a combination of a TreeVault account for cloud services and direct connections to genealogy platforms like Ancestry and FamilySearch [citation:1][citation:6]. This guide will walk you through everything you need to know about creating, accessing, and managing your various Family Tree Maker login account options.

Setting Up Your TreeVault Account
---------------------------------

The TreeVault cloud service is at the heart of the modern Family Tree Maker experience, and your Family Tree Maker login account for this service is your TreeVault account. This account enables you to back up your tree online and access it from anywhere using the Family Tree Maker Connect mobile app [citation:5][citation:8]. To create your TreeVault account, you can begin during the initial software registration process. When you open Family Tree Maker for the first time after installation, a setup wizard will appear, and you can click the Create Account button on step two [citation:1][citation:4].

If you bypass this initial step, you can still create your account later by navigating to the Connect to TreeVault Cloud Services option, which is located in the Help menu on Windows or the Family Tree Maker menu on Mac [citation:4]. Alternatively, you can click on the TreeVault icon on the main toolbar and then select Create Account from the login dialog [citation:4]. No matter which method you choose, your web browser will open the TreeVault account creation page, where you will need to fill in all required fields. It is important to use the same email address you used when purchasing Family Tree Maker, as this will help with subscription management, though you can change it later [citation:1][citation:4].

After you have successfully created your Family Tree Maker login account for TreeVault, you must log in from within the software to activate the services [citation:1][citation:4]. Once logged in, you will be prompted to set up your TreeVault Antenna tree, which is a secure cloud copy of your family tree that updates automatically as you make changes [citation:8]. This is the foundation for using the Family Tree Maker Connect app and serves as an emergency backup for your valuable research.

Accessing Other Services Through Your Software
----------------------------------------------

Beyond the TreeVault cloud service, you can also link Family Tree Maker to other major genealogy databases using your existing login credentials. This integration allows you to enrich your tree with historical records and hints from across the web. If you have an Ancestry account, you can connect it to Family Tree Maker to sync your trees and access its vast collection of records [citation:6][citation:9].

To do this, simply look for the Ancestry icon, which resembles a green leaf, in the upper-right corner of the Family Tree Maker window, click it, and select Log In to Ancestry [citation:6]. A login window will appear where you can enter your Ancestry username and password [citation:6]. Similarly, you can link your free FamilySearch account. Click the FamilySearch icon and choose Log In, then enter your FamilySearch credentials to start discovering record matches and searching its billions of names directly from the program [citation:2][citation:9].

Using the Family Tree Maker Connect App
---------------------------------------

Your TreeVault account also serves as your Family Tree Maker login account for the Family Tree Maker Connect mobile app. This free app, available for iPhone and Android, provides a real-time view of your TreeVault Antenna tree, allowing you to view and share your latest family tree from your mobile device [citation:3][citation:5]. After you have downloaded and installed the app, you can simply enter the email address and password associated with your TreeVault account to log in [citation:5]. This will load your family tree, which will be updated automatically whenever you are online.

For relatives who do not own Family Tree Maker, you can invite them to view your tree through the Connect app [citation:7]. They can create a free guest account using the email address to which the invitation was sent, and they will not need a separate Family Tree Maker login account [citation:3][citation:7]. This is a wonderful way to share your discoveries with family members, enabling them to view, explore, and even comment on the family history you have worked so hard to build.

Frequently Asked Questions
--------------------------

**Question: How do I create a Family Tree Maker login account for cloud services?**

Your cloud account is called a TreeVault account. You can create it during the initial software setup wizard or from within Family Tree Maker by clicking on the TreeVault icon and choosing Create Account. You will be directed to a webpage where you can fill in your details and create a password [citation:1][citation:4].

**Question: What should I do if I forget my TreeVault password?**

If you have forgotten your password for your Family Tree Maker login account, you can easily reset it from within Family Tree Maker. Click the TreeVault icon and select Log In. In the login window, click the Forgot password? link and follow the instructions to receive a recovery link via email [citation:1].

**Question: Can I use the same Family Tree Maker login account for Ancestry and FamilySearch?**

No, these are separate accounts. Your Family Tree Maker login account for cloud services is your TreeVault account. For Ancestry and FamilySearch, you must log in with the credentials specific to those platforms. Family Tree Maker supports connecting to both services from within the software [citation:6][citation:9].

**Question: Do I need a subscription to use TreeVault cloud services?**

When you create a TreeVault account, you will receive a free trial period to use the cloud services. After the trial ends, you will have the option to continue with a paid subscription to keep using features like the Family Tree Maker Connect app and automatic cloud backups [citation:4].

**Question: How do I log in to the Family Tree Maker Connect mobile app?**

Your login for the mobile app is the same as your Family Tree Maker login account for TreeVault. Simply enter the email address and password you used to create your TreeVault account [citation:5].
